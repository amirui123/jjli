## Intro and spread

- [Matthew Berman](https://x.com/MatthewBerman/status/2108245653843509415) - "Reverse Engineer Anything 😂 Software is done." One of the posts that set off the wide spread.
- [0xMarioNawfal](https://x.com/RoundtableSpace/status/2109165852578992582) - "Closed source is dead." Intro posted on the day REA gained 25k stars, about 920k views. An earlier [feature intro](https://x.com/RoundtableSpace/status/2107059472753664261) went out on October 5.
- [International Cyber Digest](https://x.com/IntCyberDigest/status/2108699960782774733) - Intro while REA was #1 on GitHub Trending: "Software is dead, long live software!"
- [Trending GitHub Repositories](https://x.com/trending_repos/status/2107443007217701076) - The daily trending-repo bot, reporting about 3,000 new stars in 24 hours.
- [Rohan Paul](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2108423886509683053) - Feature overview: one MCP for binaries, apps and runtime behavior.
- [Tech with Mak](https://x.com/techNmak/status/2108431092462338178) - Pitches REA as giving Claude Code a decompiler, and explains how it connects agents to Ghidra, Hopper and other tools.
- [Hasan Toor](https://x.com/hasantoxr/status/2108524682727305344) - Hand any app to AI and have it rebuild the best features inside.
- [Larry Cheng](https://x.com/larryvc/status/2109344654198018095) - A venture investor's one-line take: look at a finished app and work out how it works without the source.
- [Afiz](https://x.com/itsafiz/status/2107063919290573174) - See a feature you like and have your agent reverse-engineer it; stresses that everything runs locally.
- [Oliver Prompts](https://x.com/oliviscusAI/status/2106955446183411895) - Feature intro: decompile, trace execution, inspect runtime behavior.
- [K2S](https://x.com/k2sbhai/status/2106796616716062777) - Four steps to start: install, `rea setup`, connect your agent, give it a feature to investigate.
- [Vaishnavi](https://x.com/_vmlops/status/2106690704244420687) - An early intro post.
- [Dan Kornas](https://x.com/DanKornas/status/2106032461968761052) - Intro from October 2, one of the earliest before REA went viral. Lists evidence records, local analysis and other features.
- [CyrilXBT](https://x.com/cyrilXBT/status/2109490499321299364) - Says closed source used to mean protected and now only means slower to copy. A typical example of the hype.
- [darkzodchi](https://x.com/zodchiii/status/2109736071827476756) - Long getting-started post on REA with Claude Code: reading undocumented repos, apps without public APIs, and old .exe files whose source is lost.
- [X trending topic](https://x.com/i/trending/2108302228461080603) - X's own summary of the topic, about a GitHub tool that lets AI reverse-engineer app features from binaries.

## Chinese, Japanese and other languages

- [宝玉 (dotey): REA lets AI agents reverse-engineer software without source code](https://x.com/dotey/status/2108794629172682783) - 🇨🇳 The most complete Chinese intro: install, what it can analyze, three official showcases, where data goes, plus compliance notes and a reminder that REA has no token. About 300k views.
- [lifcc](https://x.com/mylifcc/status/2108940999527690525) - 🇨🇳 On an open-source project gaining 20k+ stars in a day, and what it means that Claude Code or Codex can now study binaries and rebuild features.
- [婷 (shijianzhongg)](https://x.com/shijianzhongg/status/2107979591348543520) - 🇨🇳 Calls it the day's viral open-source project on GitHub and lists three uses: finding hidden features, checking for backdoors, learning how big companies build things. About 170k views.
- [TechVerser](https://x.com/realfxw/status/2106734209473581308) - 🇨🇳 Says AI is rewriting the rules of low-level reverse engineering, and explains how REA packages the toolchain as an agent add-on.
- [Khazix](https://x.com/Khazix0918/status/2109501787736813663) - 🇨🇳 Says AI has started reverse-engineering everything, so every app on your computer is about to be forced open source.
- [吉川孝志 (author of *マルウエアの教科書*)](https://x.com/MalwareBibleJP/status/2108736281647931717) - 🇯🇵 A malware analyst's and defender's view: dependencies, static versus runtime analysis, where data goes and the disclaimer, point by point.
- [midudev](https://x.com/midudev/status/2107834472091598854) - 🇪🇸 Says everyone is using AI to reverse-engineer programs and games. The most-shared post in Spanish-speaking developer circles, about 150k views.
- [bearstech](https://x.com/bearstech/status/2109679666105655457) / [actu_ia](https://x.com/actu_ia/status/2107107539485958502) - 🇫🇷 Short intros in French.

## Official accounts

- [@morluto](https://x.com/morluto) - REA's author. In the [rea 6.4 release post](https://x.com/morluto/status/2109138696008327226) they say it is much faster and share [morluto/flameox](https://github.com/morluto/flameox), the profiler used for that work.
- [@reatools](https://x.com/reatools) - The official account. It reposted the author's line "the homebrew computer club era of technology is back".

## Roundups

REA often appears in "open-source tools that replace paid software" roundups, next to other viral repos.

- [Vaibhav Sisinty: what people built with it this week](https://x.com/VaibhavSisinty/status/2109618846453977359) - A thread of cases: a Steam game decompiled, the first-gen Apple TV remote, an AC remote, a Plex client for LG TVs and more. Most of the original posts don't say REA was used; they belong to the [wider AI reverse-engineering wave](../README.md#the-wider-wave-without-confirmed-rea).
- [xiangxiang103's REA case roundup](https://x.com/xiangxiang103/status/2109813885641576795) - 🇨🇳 A batch of user cases collected on X.
- [brenz.](https://x.com/brenzhills/status/2108983461230845978) - 🇪🇸 Ten repos blowing up this month, with REA at #4 next to VoiceStudio, ppt-master, gods-eye-view, diagram-design and paperclip.
- [Chris Pirillo](https://x.com/ChrisPirillo/status/2109843806187303400) - Vibe-coding and AI news roundup for October 12, listing REA next to context-mode, diagram-design and others.
- [govin.eth｜G哥](https://x.com/goan999999/status/2109544711400857688) - 🇨🇳 Six open-source tools on GitHub that spell the end for paid software: PPT Master, VoiceStudio, God's Eye View, Diagram Design, Paperclip and ECC. It doesn't mention REA, but it is the same batch of projects and the same story as the two posts above.

## Controversy

- [codila](https://x.com/0xCodila/status/2109623431763292649) - "REA is most dangerous thing on the internet right now." Pitches it for copying paid features for free, a typical example of what the controversy is about (see [Legal and controversy](../README.md#legal-and-controversy)).
- [刘小排 (bourneliu66)](https://x.com/bourneliu66/status/2109882364705038422) - 🇨🇳 Argues REA is just hype: not a new reverse-engineering technique, only a way for agents to call existing tools, overhyped by influencers. A representative counterpoint.
- [Jaden Williams](https://x.com/JadenJTW/status/2106890896981508329) - Reposted an early intro with only "God help us all."
