# Human-Centered Brand Strategy — Don Norman (The Design of Everyday Things)

## Human-Centered Design (HCD) Applied to Branding

### The Core Principle
- Design for REAL humans, not idealized users
- Observe how people actually interact with the brand (not how you hope they will)
- When the brand confuses people, the brand is wrong — not the people

### The 7 Stages of Action (Brand Experience)
1. **Goal**: What does the customer want to achieve? (Job to be done)
2. **Plan**: How do they intend to interact with the brand?
3. **Specify**: What specific action do they take? (Visit website, enter store, open app)
4. **Perform**: They execute the action
5. **Perceive**: What do they see/hear/feel? (Brand touchpoint experience)
6. **Interpret**: What does the experience mean to them? (Brand = gut feeling)
7. **Compare**: Did the experience match their goal? (Satisfaction or frustration)

Design every brand touchpoint to succeed at all 7 stages.

### Gulf of Execution & Gulf of Evaluation
- **Gulf of Execution**: The gap between what the customer wants to do and what the brand allows them to do → Make brand interactions intuitive
- **Gulf of Evaluation**: The gap between what happens and what the customer understands → Make brand communication clear

## Key Design Principles for Brand Strategy

### Affordances & Signifiers
- **Affordance**: What the brand enables the customer to do
- **Signifier**: What tells the customer what the brand enables
- Brand touchpoints must clearly SIGNAL what the brand offers
- If customers don't understand what the brand does in 5 seconds, the signifiers are failing

### Mapping
- The relationship between the brand promise and the brand experience must be natural
- If the brand promises "simplicity", every touchpoint must feel simple
- Mismatched mapping (premium promise, cheap experience) destroys trust instantly

### Feedback
- Every customer action should get immediate brand response
- Digital: click → visual confirmation. Physical: purchase → thank you note.
- Lack of feedback = customer anxiety → "Did it work? Does the brand care?"

### Constraints
- Good design uses constraints to prevent errors
- Good branding uses constraints to prevent off-brand experiences
- Brand guidelines ARE constraints — they prevent inconsistency

### Error Handling
- When things go wrong (and they will), the brand experience during error recovery defines loyalty
- "The brand isn't judged by its best moments — it's judged by its worst"
- Design elegant failure states: 404 pages, out-of-stock messages, service disruptions

## Conceptual Models
- Customers build a **mental model** of the brand from every interaction
- The brand team's model and the customer's model MUST align
- If there's a gap between how the team thinks the brand works and how customers experience it → The Brand Gap (Neumeier)
- Simplify the conceptual model: "We help [persona] do [job] by [unique approach]"

## Strategic Application
- Apply HCD to every brand touchpoint: observe, prototype, test, iterate
- Map the customer journey through the 7 stages of action
- Close the gulfs of execution and evaluation at every touchpoint
- Design for error: the brand's response to problems IS the brand experience
- The customer's mental model of the brand is THE brand — align with it or change it
