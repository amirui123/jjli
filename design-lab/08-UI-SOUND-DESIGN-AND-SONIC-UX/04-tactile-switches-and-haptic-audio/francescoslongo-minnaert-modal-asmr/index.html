Hush

How to use Hush

Put on headphones: sounds are placed around your head, and that only works when each ear gets its own channel.

Getting started

Press Start listening. Pink noise plays by default. The same button then pauses and resumes everything.

Use the Section menu above the list to move between groups of sounds. A green dot marks sections where something is playing. Switch a sound on with the toggle next to its name.

The Volume slider under the map sets the overall level.

Scenes and Surprise me

In Scenes, press Play on a ready-made mix, or build your own and press Save current mix. A scene keeps which sounds are on, all their settings, the room, the key and the patterns.

Copy link puts the mix in a web link: anyone who opens it gets the same mix. Your own files (Your sounds) aren't part of scenes, since they never leave your device.

Surprise me, next to Start listening, puts together a random, balanced mix: a background layer and a few sounds spread around you.

Chaos mode, next to Surprise me, does a new Surprise mix over and over; Chaos changes, under the volume, sets how often, from every 5 seconds to every 5 minutes. Each change fades out and back in. Press Chaos mode again to stop and keep the current mix.

Room, sleep timer and recording
  • Room puts all sounds in a space: a small room, a bathroom, a concert hall or outdoors. Each sound's Reverb slider sets how much of it reaches the room.
  • Sleep timer fades everything out slowly over the last few minutes and then pauses. It counts listening time, so pausing pauses it too.
  • Record saves what you hear to an audio file; press again to stop and download it.
On your phone

Play and pause also appear on the lock screen and in the notification area, and sounds keep playing with the screen off in most browsers.

When Hush is opened from a website, you can install it: use Install app if it appears, or "Add to Home Screen" in the browser's menu. Once installed it also opens without a connection.

Now playing

Lists every sound that is on, with its own volume and an Off button. Click a sound's name to jump to its settings.

Patterns moving that sound appear under it, with their curve, current value and speed. Turn everything off and Stop all patterns are at the bottom.

Settings of a sound

Open Settings under a sound's name. Every sound has:

  • Volume: how loud it is in the mix.
  • Movement: still, circling around you, sweeping from ear to ear, or drifting.
  • Direction and Distance: where it is. Closer sounds get louder, warmer and more clearly on one side.
  • Movement speed: how fast it moves when it isn't still.
  • Reverb: how much of it you hear reflected by the room.

Most sounds also have their own controls, such as rate, material or size.

The map

The circle is a view from above: your head is in the middle, facing up. Each coloured dot is a sound, and the rings mark 25 cm, 50 cm, 1 m and 2.5 m.

Drag a dot to move that sound; its Direction and Distance update as you go. A moving sound becomes still when you grab it. Sounds set to "All around" are drawn as a ring and can't be dragged.

Filter

Inside a sound's settings, Filter works like the filters used in electronics:

  • High-pass removes the low end (rumble, boominess).
  • Low-pass removes the high end (hiss, sharpness).
  • Both together keep only a band in between.
  • Slope sets how steeply it cuts: 12 or 24 dB per octave.
  • Resonance adds a peak at the cut-off.

The plot shows what the filter does to each frequency.

Instruments

All instruments play in the Key and Scale chosen at the top of the Instruments section, so any combination sounds good together. Changing them retunes held notes smoothly.

Patterns

A pattern makes a setting move by itself, over and over.

  • Record: press Record, move the slider, press Stop.
  • Generate: pick a shape (sine, triangle, ramps, square, pulse, breathing, or a random one), its length, and the lowest and highest values.
  • Use a pattern: choose the pattern, the sound, the setting to move and a speed, then press Apply.

Moving a sound's direction with a pattern is a nice way to make it travel around you. Patterns are saved in this browser.

Your sounds

Load an audio or video file into one of the three slots. It is analysed on the spot: the report shows how many separate sounds it found, their rhythm, how quickly they fade, their brightness and the pitches they ring at.

  • Play: "The recording" uses sounds cut from your file. "Synthesised copy" rebuilds the sound from the measurements only, without any of the recording.
  • Treat as: separate sounds, or a continuous texture that loops.
  • Timing, Tempo, Pitch and Variation change how it plays back.

Files never leave your device and are gone after a reload. If a video won't load, export its audio as .wav or .mp3.
