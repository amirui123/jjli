# UI SFX - Sonic Personalities Reference (12 Packs)

UI SFX allows instantly changing the whole sonic identity of the product without altering any interaction code.

## 1. `minimal`
* **Character** : Dry, precise, almost invisible.
* **Acoustics** : Sine waves, fast attack (4ms), rapid decay, minimal noise.
* **Best for** : SaaS, enterprise dashboards, developer tools, productivity suites.

## 2. `soft`
* **Character** : Rounded felt, warm and reassuring.
* **Acoustics** : Triangle waves, gentle attack (12ms), mild echo.
* **Best for** : Mobile apps, wellness, healthcare, friendly B2C SaaS.

## 3. `glass`
* **Character** : Bright, crystalline, and premium.
* **Acoustics** : High pitch, sine wave with rich upper harmonics, subtle echo.
* **Best for** : Fintech, luxury eCommerce, high-end media, portfolio sites.

## 4. `arcade`
* **Character** : Chunky pixels and cheerful voltage.
* **Acoustics** : Square wave, 8-bit quantization, distinct semi-tone steps.
* **Best for** : Games, gamified learning, streaks, youth platforms.

## 5. `mechanical`
* **Character** : Switches, relays, and firm physical detents.
* **Acoustics** : Triangle waves with transient noise and low pitch.
* **Best for** : Devtools, hardware management, industrial UI, terminal emulators.

## 6. `organic`
* **Character** : Wood, water, breath, and small stones.
* **Acoustics** : Dual-pitch detuned acoustic textures, natural resonance.
* **Best for** : Education, children's apps, nature apps, calm games.

## 7. `scifi`
* **Character** : Clean holographic pings with restrained digital shimmer.
* **Acoustics** : Frequency modulation (FM synthesis), crisp synthetic transients.
* **Best for** : AI tools, chatbots, spatial UI, sci-fi games.

## 8. `rubber`
* **Character** : Tactile elastic taps with a quick, friendly rebound.
* **Acoustics** : Triangle wave with damped frequency oscillation (elastic bounce).
* **Best for** : Playful mobile apps, casual games, creative tools.

## 9. `cinematic`
* **Character** : Deep impacts, polished tails, and quiet scale.
* **Acoustics** : Sub-bass octaves (-24 semitones), rich reverberation.
* **Best for** : Premium streaming, dramatic game moments, video portals.

## 10. `studio`
* **Character** : Tactile editing precision with warm cinematic restraint.
* **Acoustics** : Console click transients, balanced frequencies.
* **Best for** : Video and audio editing suites, creative design tools.

## 11. `zen`
* **Character** : Pure tones, dry wood, and brief washi paper detail.
* **Acoustics** : Washi paper brush, dry wood click, and singing bowl chiming textures.
* **Best for** : Mindfulness, meditation, reading, distraction-free writing.

## 12. `dreamy`
* **Character** : Airy blooms, soft light, and slow sparkle.
* **Acoustics** : Soft attack (20ms), upper octave shimmer (+12 semitones), stereo echo.
* **Best for** : Wellness, ambient audio, creative portfolios, sleep apps.
