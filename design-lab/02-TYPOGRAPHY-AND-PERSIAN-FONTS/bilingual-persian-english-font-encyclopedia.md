# Complete Persian, Arabic & English Font Encyclopedia (93 Bundled Families)

## 1. Overview
Every font family listed in `src/data/persianFontsCatalog.ts` (`93` families total) is backed by a real, downloadable `.woff2`/`.woff`/`.ttf` binary inside `/public/fonts/` and verified by `scripts/generate-font-files.mjs`.
